<p>Onda is a minimal home screen built around a single vertical list of your favorite apps and an alphabet scroller on the screen edge.</p>
<ul>
<li>Alphabet wave scroller on both screen edges (one side invisible) to reach any app with one thumb</li>
<li>Notifications shown right under each favorite, with swipe to dismiss</li>
<li>Quick app panel with a right swipe: shortcuts, notifications and a widget of that app</li>
<li>Folders that hold apps and widgets</li>
<li>Material You colors, themed icons and third-party icon packs</li>
<li>Search for apps, contacts and the web, with a built-in calculator</li>
<li>Music card, next calendar event, double-tap to lock</li>
<li>Configurable gestures, hidden apps, backup and restore</li>
</ul>
<p>No ads, no tracking, no Google Play Services. Nothing leaves your device.</p>
